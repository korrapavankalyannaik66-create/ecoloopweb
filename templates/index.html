Who We Are

About EcoLoop Platform

🏛️ Govt Regulated Oversight

Every transaction is monitored and verified by registered Government Officers to ensure zero toxic waste dumping.

🏭 Certified Recyclers

We connect sellers directly with authorised recycling companies, guaranteeing proper extraction of precious metals.
